    CP and EP

    CP caps at 200. S-Crafts and S-Breaks need 100 CP and spend 100; at 200 CP they can be empowered and spend all 200. A character needs a regular turn between S-Crafts. Crafts and arts spend their CP/EP cost when you confirm them, and HEAL / EP / CP bonuses apply to whoever takes that turn.

    AT bonuses belong to timeline positions, not to people. Delaying or hastening a unit moves it onto a different bonus.
